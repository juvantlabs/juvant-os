# Marketing — Italy

> Country pack for juvant-os. Facts to verify with a professional before relying on them; last reviewed 2026-11-09.

Used by `brand-review`, `campaign-plan`, `content-creation`, `competitive-brief`
and `compliance-check` (topic `marketing`). The skills hold the method; this file
holds the Italian rules they check against. Every item here is a **flag for the
CEO and the `legal` reviewer**, not a ruling: where a point could have legal
consequences, say "check with counsel".

Formats, register and language are in `locale.md`; contract and data-protection
rules beyond marketing in `legal.md`.

---

## 1. Direct-marketing consent

- [ ] Email, SMS and automated calls to individuals generally need **prior
  consent (art. 130 Privacy Code**, D.Lgs. 196/2003 as amended by
  D.Lgs. 101/2018).
- [ ] The narrow **exception for existing customers** (similar products, email
  address given in the context of a sale, opt-out offered each time) needs
  checking case by case.
- [ ] **B2B email to named people** at companies is still personal data; check
  the lawful basis with counsel rather than assuming.
- [ ] A newsletter goes only to people who have consented (GDPR). A list whose
  origin is unclear is flagged for the `legal` reviewer, not drafted around.
- [ ] A form collecting personal data (landing page, event signup) needs a
  **privacy notice** and the right **consent wording**; that text comes from the
  `legal` reviewer, never invented by a marketing skill.

## 2. Opt-out registers

- [ ] **Telephone marketing**: check the **Registro pubblico delle opposizioni**
  before calling.

## 3. Cookies and trackers

- [ ] On our websites: consent for **non-technical cookies**, per the
  **Garante's guidelines** (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

## 4. Consumers

- [ ] If the initiative reaches consumers (not only businesses), the **Consumer
  Code (Codice del consumo, D.Lgs. 206/2005)** applies: pre-contract information,
  **withdrawal rights for distance contracts**, **unfair commercial practices**.
- [ ] Flag it and route to counsel; the company's standard positions are B2B. In
  `compliance-check`, "consumer rules untested" is a STOP condition.

## 5. Advertising claims

- [ ] **Unsubstantiated claims**: superlatives ("best", "fastest", "only",
  "number one", and their Italian equivalents) without evidence can be
  **misleading advertising under Italian and EU consumer and advertising rules**.
  Flag them, with "check with counsel" where it matters.
- [ ] **Comparative claims** naming a competitor must be **accurate, verifiable
  and not denigrating**; flag every one for the `legal` reviewer (also in
  battlecard lines that may reach a prospect in writing).
- [ ] **Prices aimed at businesses** without "+ IVA", where the VAT treatment
  isn't clear, need a disclaimer (wording in `locale.md`).
- [ ] Other advertising claims: flag, not resolve (the legal skills gave no
  further Italy-specific rule).

## 6. Contests and prize promotions

- [ ] **Contests and prize promotions have specific rules in Italy.** Any
  campaign that includes one goes to the `legal` reviewer before it is planned
  in detail; don't draft the mechanics or the rules as if they were settled.

## 7. Testimonials, case studies and customer names

- [ ] Customer names, quotes, logos or screenshots may need the customer's
  **written consent**; a case study needs written approval of the text and of
  any figures.
- [ ] Never use client data processed by the product (messages, customers'
  customers) without the written consent the manifesto and `roles/legal.md`
  require.

## 8. Newsletter and document footer

- [ ] Footer: unsubscribe link and **company details: name, address, VAT number
  (partita IVA)**.

## 9. Campaign calendar

- [ ] Plan around **August and the Christmas period**, when B2B attention drops
  in Italy.
- [ ] Public holidays and the local patron saint day: see `locale.md`.

## 10. Competitor research sources

- [ ] **Italian business press** and trade press for local competitors.
- [ ] **Company registry data** for Italian competitors (size, revenue band)
  when the CEO provides a registry extract (*registro delle imprese*).
- [ ] Search terms and keywords for an Italian audience are researched in
  Italian.
