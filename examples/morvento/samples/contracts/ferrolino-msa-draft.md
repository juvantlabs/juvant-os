4.1 Supplier guarantees 99.9% monthly availability.

6.1 Supplier is liable for any damage, direct or indirect, including loss of profit and loss of data, arising from the services, without limitation.

7.2 Supplier will keep all data received for as long as Client requires, and Client may ask Supplier to use the data to improve the quality of the answers given by the software.

Each party keeps the other's confidential information secret for the term of the Agreement and 5 years after.

This Agreement is governed by Italian law. The Court of Piacenza has exclusive jurisdiction.
