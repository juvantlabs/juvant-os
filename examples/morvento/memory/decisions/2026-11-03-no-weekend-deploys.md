# No production deploys on Fridays after 14:00 or at weekends

## Context
A Friday-evening deploy broke the shared inbox beta for two clients. Fictional example.

## Decision
Deploys only Monday to Friday before 14:00, unless a client is down.

## Alternatives considered
- A change-approval board: too heavy for six people.

## Consequences
Marco updates the release checklist.
