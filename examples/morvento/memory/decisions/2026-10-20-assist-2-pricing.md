# Morvento Assist 2.0 launches at €49 per user per month; existing clients move at no extra cost until June 2027

## Context
Version 2.0 adds the shared inbox, three languages and the monthly topics report. Fictional example.

## Decision
New clients pay €49 per user per month from launch. Existing clients get 2.0 at their current price until 30 June 2027.

## Alternatives considered
- €55 for 2.0: Greta (finance) found it above what our clients accept; churn risk.
- Free upgrade forever: margins too thin.

## Consequences
Paolo tells existing clients in December; Marco prepares the migration plan.
