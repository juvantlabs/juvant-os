# Brand rules (Morvento)

Fictional example.

- Name: always "Morvento", never in capitals. The product is "Morvento Assist".
- Voice: direct, warm, concrete (see the manifesto). Speak to small business owners, not to IT departments.
- Claims we use: "answers drafted in seconds, approved by your team"; "your customers' data stays in the EU".
- Claims we never use: "replaces your support team", "never wrong", "revolutionary".
- Channels: LinkedIn and the newsletter (about 1,200 subscribers); local events in Bologna and Milan.
