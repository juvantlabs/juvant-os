# Morvento Assist (product facts)

Fictional example.

- An AI assistant that drafts answers to customer messages (email, web chat) for small companies. A person on the client's team approves or edits every answer before it goes out, unless the client enables auto-replies for simple questions.
- Version 2.0 (launch planned for January 2027): a shared inbox, answers in Italian, English and German, and a monthly report of the topics customers ask about.
- Pricing: €49 per user per month; setup project from €3,000.
- Data: messages are processed in the EU (Frankfurt region of the cloud provider) and deleted 90 days after the end of the contract. No training on client data.
- Customers: about 40 small companies, mostly retail and services in Emilia-Romagna and Lombardy.
- What it doesn't do: it doesn't replace the support team, it doesn't make refunds or decisions, and it can be wrong; the client's team stays in charge.
