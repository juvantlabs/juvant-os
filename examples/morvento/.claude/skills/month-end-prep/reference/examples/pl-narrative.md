# Month in brief — worked example

## Scenario

**Company:** Morvento, six-person B2B software company (fictional).

**Month:** October 2026

**Figures** (receivables list; bank figures illustrative):
- Invoiced: EUR 5,130 (2026/096 Ottica Brenta EUR 3,660; 2026/099 Pasticceria Gelsi EUR 1,470). September: EUR 12,200.
- Collected: EUR 610 (2026/091 Agriturismo Le Querce, paid 27 Oct, two days early).
- Paid out: EUR 32,900 (net salaries EUR 16,500, monthly tax payment of 16 Oct EUR 9,800, rent and subscriptions EUR 3,700, suppliers EUR 2,900).
- Bank: EUR 147,990 on 1 Oct → EUR 115,700 on 31 Oct.
- Overdue at 31 Oct: Studio Varenna, EUR 7,320 across two invoices, oldest 37 days late.
- Minimum buffer (`roles/finance.md`): EUR 90,000.

---

## Example note

> **October: EUR 5,130 invoiced, EUR 610 collected, cash down EUR 32,290 to EUR 115,700.**
>
> Invoicing was less than half of September's EUR 12,200: two invoices, Ottica Brenta and Pasticceria Gelsi. Ferrolino Logistica, last invoiced in September for EUR 9,150, had no invoice this month. Collections were thin because September's big invoice (Ferrolino) was paid early, on 30 September, and October's invoices fall due in November.
>
> Outflows were the usual month: salaries, the monthly tax payment and rent made up EUR 30,000 of the EUR 32,900. Suppliers were EUR 2,900, in line with September.
>
> Cash is EUR 25,700 above the EUR 90,000 buffer. That margin covers less than one month of fixed costs, and December carries the 13th month.
>
> **Watch list for November:** (1) Studio Varenna, EUR 7,320 overdue; the oldest invoice reaches the CEO-call step on 8 November. (2) Whether Ferrolino's work continues: confirm whether an invoice is due. (3) The second income-tax advance at end of November (e.g. the Italian calendar): amount from the accountant.
>
> *This is a cash view from the bank statement and the receivables list, not a profit and loss.*

---

## What makes this note work

- Opens with the headline numbers, not a preamble.
- Names clients where it matters (the missing Ferrolino invoice, the overdue Varenna).
- Explains low collections by timing, with the dates, instead of calling it "a slow month".
- Puts cash against the company's own buffer rule.
- Watch list is specific and dated.
- Says what it is not: a cash view, not a P&L.
