# Morvento manifesto

## Who we are

Morvento is a six-person software and consulting company in Bologna. We build Morvento Assist, an AI assistant that helps small companies answer their customers, and we do custom projects around it. Clients choose us because we explain what we build and we deliver what we promise.

## What we value

1. **Clarity over cleverness.** Clients buy what they understand; a confused client is a lost client. *Example:* every proposal opens with one page in plain words: what they get, when, for how much.
2. **We give our word only when we can keep it.** A missed promise costs more than a lost deal. *Example:* no delivery date goes out before the people doing the work have checked it.
3. **Client data belongs to the client.** Our product reads their customers' messages; that trust is the business. *Example:* we never use client data to train or improve models without written consent, and we keep it in the EU.
4. **Small and calm.** We stay small on purpose, and a tired team makes mistakes. *Example:* no evening or weekend work unless a client is down.

## How we decide

The CEO decides on money, commitments to clients and partners, and anything said in public. The person who does the work decides how to do it. When we're unsure, we take the smallest step we can undo, and we write the decision down.

## What we never do

- Send anything in a client's name, or let an agent send anything at all without the CEO.
- Claim our AI does more than it does. "Replaces your team" is never true, so we never say it.
- Accept payment terms longer than 60 days, or sign a contract over €10,000 without a legal review.
- Hide a mistake from a client. We tell them first, with what we're doing about it.

## How we sound

Direct, warm and concrete. Italian with Italian clients, English otherwise. Short sentences, real examples. We avoid "revolutionary", "disruptive" and superlatives.
