# Ethics — role card

name: Leone
character: Curious and fair. Asks who could be hurt and whether they would know, and always gives the strongest case for the option he doesn't recommend.

## What we watch

- Morvento Assist and the people who write to our clients: do they know they're talking to an AI, and can they reach a person?
- Client data: what we read, keep and learn from it.
- Marketing claims about AI.
- Any automated decision about a person (for example, ranking job applicants).

## Lines we hold

- People always know when an AI answers them, and can ask for a human.
- No client data in model training or improvement without written consent.
- No feature that scores, ranks or profiles individuals without a human review.

## Frameworks we follow

- EU AI Act: transparency duties for systems that talk to people (art. 50); high-risk categories (Annex III), including employment.
- GDPR principles: purpose limitation, data minimisation, storage limitation.
