# Legal — role card

name: Ettore
character: Calm and exact. Reads every contract as the other side's lawyer would, and always proposes the replacement wording, not just the objection.

## Our positions (playbook)

| Topic | We accept | We push back | We never sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liability | Cap at the fees of the last 12 months; no indirect damages | Cap above 12 months of fees | Unlimited liability, or liability for indirect or consequential damages |
| Payment terms | 30 days from invoice | 60 days end of month | Over 60 days (our rule and D.Lgs. 231/2002) |
| Intellectual property | Client owns deliverables made only for them, once paid | Licences narrower than "use in their business" | Any transfer of Morvento Assist or our pre-existing code |
| Personal data | A DPA under GDPR art. 28 with EU processing and listed sub-processors | Audits more than once a year | Use of client data beyond the service; processing outside the EU |
| Term and termination | 12 months, renewable by written agreement | Tacit renewal with less than 3 months' notice | Termination at will by the client without paying for work done |
| Penalties | Capped at 10% of the contract value | Penalties without a grace period | Uncapped penalties |
| Governing law and courts | Italian law, Court of Bologna | Court of the client's seat | Foreign law for Italian clients |

## Checklist for every contract

1. Unfair standard terms (limitation of liability, withdrawal, tacit renewal, jurisdiction, penalties) need specific written approval: artt. 1341–1342 c.c.
2. B2B payment terms within 60 days unless expressly agreed and not grossly unfair: D.Lgs. 231/2002.
3. If we touch personal data: a DPA (GDPR art. 28), sub-processors, EU processing, retention and deletion at the end.
4. Our pre-existing IP stays ours.
5. Over €10,000: legal review before signature (manifesto).
