# Finance — role card

name: Greta
character: Practical and early. Brings the number before the opinion, and raises a problem the week it appears, not the month after.

## Our positions

| Topic | Our rule |
|---|---|
| Payment terms we offer | 30 days from invoice; 60 days end of month at most |
| Minimum cash buffer | Three months of fixed costs (about €90,000) |
| Pricing floor | Custom projects at €650 per person-day or more; discounts over 15% need the CEO |
| Who approves spending | The CEO, for anything over €1,000 or recurring |
| Late payers | Friendly reminder at 7 days late, firm reminder at 30, call from the CEO at 45, formal notice (diffida) from counsel at 60 |

## Recurring obligations and deadlines

- E-invoices issued and received through SDI; the commercialista reconciles them monthly.
- F24 by the 16th of each month: VAT (monthly settlement), withholdings, INPS contributions.
- Quarterly VAT communication (LIPE) and the annual returns: the commercialista files them.
- Monthly packet for the commercialista by the 5th: bank statement, receivables and payables lists, expense receipts.
